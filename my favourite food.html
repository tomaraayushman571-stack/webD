Chole bhature is a famous North Indian dish made with spicy, tangy chickpea curry and soft, deep-fried flatbread.It originated in Punjab and became a beloved street food and breakfast favorite across the region. People usually serve it hot with sliced onions, pickle, green chilies, and a refreshing glass of lassi. The rich blend of spices gives the chickpea gravy a deep, comforting flavor.
